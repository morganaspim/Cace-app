## Important mathematics
- Four-valued evidence `(t,f) ∈ {0,1}²` can express contradictory reports without logically exploding.
- Bayesian `P(H_i|E)=P(E|H_i)P(H_i)/Σ_j P(E|H_j)P(H_j)` requires explicit model and independent/dependence-aware evidence; numbers without likelihoods are not calibrated confidence.
- Fuzzy group population estimator `Σ_i w_i μ_k(x_i)` requires valid survey design and defined membership.
- Pareto dominance means nonworse on every comparable objective and better on at least one; non-dominance is a real tradeoff, not an error.
- DFT `X_k=Σ_n x_n exp(-2πikn/N)`; aliasing, calibration and noise preclude intent attribution from peaks alone.
- `G_{μν}+Λg_{μν}=8πG T_{μν}/c⁴` is GR with explicit matter assumptions; a numeric wave/eigenmode is not a stable physical wormhole.
- `2^M` possible capability subsets (before type, cost, permission pruning) explains why typed graph routing beats naive all-persona activation.
