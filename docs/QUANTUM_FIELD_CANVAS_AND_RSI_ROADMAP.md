# Quantum Unified Field Theory Canvas and RSI Optimization Roadmap

CACE should support quantum unified field theory research as a rigorous tool-building program, not as an AI-declared theory. The canvas can host fields, particles, atoms, molecules, gauge objects, lattice objects, operators, and coordinate-free mathematical objects. Scientific promotion requires CACE validation and formal proof linkage where applicable.

## GUI principles

- Geometry canvas: Desmos-inspired visual manipulation, sliders, coordinate overlays, animated transformations, and immediate visual feedback.
- Algebra canvas: MATLAB-inspired equation entry, clean symbolic notation, reusable variables, parameter sweeps, and structured outputs.
- Physical canvas: atoms, molecules, proteins, metamaterial cells, and field objects with visible forces and energy proposals.
- Custom wizards: guided creation of equations, molecules, materials, coordinate systems, graph workflows, and proof obligations.

## Forces and physical objects

Physical objects should show force vectors from sliders or computed modules. Current sliders are visualization/proposal controls for gravity, electric, magnetic, bond, and external fields. They are not evidence until backed by canonical CACE physics modules.

## Metamaterial search

Metamaterial search should combine local hypothesis libraries, parameterized geometry, field equations, spectra, stability checks, and proof obligations. The GUI may import objects through approved APIs. The AI group remains `LOCAL_ZERO_CONTACT` and can play with local objects only when `AI canvas play` is enabled.

## Quantum unified field theory direction

Initial object families:

- scalar, vector, spinor, tensor, and gauge-field objects;
- Hamiltonian, Lagrangian, action, symmetry, and constraint objects;
- lattice/gauge discretizations with continuum-limit metadata;
- operator algebra and spectrum objects;
- exact/interval numerical certificates;
- Lean-exported proof obligations for identities and invariants.

## Ranked RSI proof optimization routes

1. Typed tool contracts and proof-obligation extraction.
2. CPU/GPU and implementation equivalence certificates.
3. Interval and residual error enclosures.
4. Lean linkage and proof-minimization workflow.
5. Benchmark-before-rewrite optimization harness.

Every recursive self-improvement step must pass tests, validation, review, and proof linkage before promotion.
