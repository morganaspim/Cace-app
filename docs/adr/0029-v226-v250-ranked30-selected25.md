# ADR 0029 — v2.26–v2.50 ranked 30 / selected 25

Thirty candidates were ranked by expected leverage from the v2.25 baseline. The top 25
were selected one-to-one for v2.26–v2.50. The five deferred candidates are polygonal/VEM,
isogeometric analysis, fractional/nonlocal PDEs, nonlinear eigenproblems, and quantum-PDE
specialization.

Selection strongly favors closing certification loops and turning existing CACE theory
into reusable workflows: higher-order and 3D equilibrated flux, guaranteed goal and
parabolic bounds, nonlinear validation, Stokes/Maxwell foundations, spectral enclosure,
certified reduced basis, multilevel solvers, anisotropic/hp adaptivity, uncertainty, and
selected new PDE classes.

Reference fixtures and theorem contracts remain explicitly narrower than production
whole-family solvers. No reviewed theorem is promoted to machine-formal evidence.
