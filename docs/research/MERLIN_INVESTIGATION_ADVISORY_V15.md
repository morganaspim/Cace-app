## Observing blockage vs establishing censorship

| Report | Evidentiary interpretation |
|---|---|
| Unavailable tool, timeout, 404 or incomplete source | Cause unknown; do not infer censorship or intent |
| Provider explicitly reports a content restriction, caller supplies receipt | Reported restriction; authenticity unconfirmed |
| Trusted host separately confirms identified provider receipt | Explicit restriction observed within scope; motive still unestablished |
| Hidden deliberate manipulation | Requires independent evidence; never inferred by this module |

Preserve the request, source locator (without private data), observed response class, timestamp, provider receipt when present and whether authenticated. Differentiate service policy, moderation, ordinary access controls, unavailability and missing evidence. Do not bypass authorization or use covert circumvention.

## Iterative improvement protocol

1. Inspect objective, constraints, sources, acceptance, prior attempts and exact current source.
2. Compare hypotheses including counterevidence and OTHER_UNMODELED.
3. Choose critical lenses, trusted registered tools and the smallest discriminating authorized step.
4. Capture host-observed progress/error receipts, not AI-declared verification.
5. If action/state fingerprints are unchanged, HOLD and replan rather than repeat.
6. If new evidence or another authorized experiment changes inquiry, propose the next reviewed step.
7. Stop on budget exhaustion, missing permission, no meaningful progress or unresolved external dependency. Record the new learning and its limits.
