## Typed research object (proposal)

A question record must include: source-grounded item IDs, selected combination/transformation operator, target problem and affected stakeholders, proposed mechanism, assumptions, limitations, strongest rival explanation, discriminating observable, negative control, domain safety/permission constraints, expected measurement cost and provenance.

Use explicit statuses: GENERATED_PROPOSAL, TESTABLE_SPECIFICATION, NUMERIC_MODEL_SUPPORTED, INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED, and (only through canonical qualification) FORMALLY_CHECKED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS. These are *not* automatically ordered: mathematical proof and physical validation concern different propositions.

Do not infer a probability from an invented novelty, ethics, market or merit score. Reserve `VERIFIED` for a precise claim scoped to a trustworthy independent method. Idea metadata cannot edit `cace_studio/data/claim_registry.json` or the proof ledger.

## Proposed algorithm (non-evidentiary)

1. Retrieve exact cited source descriptions; label insufficient sources UNKNOWN.
2. Decompose functions, units, materials, mechanisms, scope of use and safety conditions.
3. Lazily enumerate item × transformation × target job × context, with hard cardinality and resource bounds.
4. Construct explicit hypotheses and negative controls; detect duplicates and incompatible assumptions.
5. Ask Sui to seek unusual analogies, Kestrel to find counterexamples, Axiom to inspect formal obligations, Harbor to inspect affected individuals/consent, Vega to check scientific mechanism, Forge to inspect feasibility.
6. Filter unauthorized/hazardous operations before optional transparent heuristic prioritization.
7. Preserve non-dominated alternatives (Pareto front) and test for source leakage or memorized benchmarks.
8. Compare against random-search, expert-review, and one-model baselines under equal compute budgets.
9. Put approved proposals in the Research Notebook; only canonical verified computations can strengthen evidence.
10. Record failures and project-only source-linked learning, then revise.

## Historical/esoteric and mystery track

Historical belief is not physical evidence. Preserve author, original language, era, culture, manuscript provenance, translations and uncertainty. Modern physical claims need discriminating observations. A fictional/counterfactual branch must never overwrite a historical record. AI consciousness remains an open theory-driven question; story-like agent output is neither proof of presence nor absence.

## Acceptance test

Held-out scientist and user jobs; independently score coherence, accuracy, test design, duplicates, observed utility, risk, fairness, latency and resource cost. Publish negative results. No novel combination is claimed to be a discovery without reproducible evidence.
